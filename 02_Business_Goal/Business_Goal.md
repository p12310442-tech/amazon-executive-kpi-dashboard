**Project Overview**

The Amazon Executive Leadership KPI Dashboard project focuses on creating a centralized business intelligence solution that provides leadership with visibility into critical business performance indicators.

The dashboard transforms raw business data into meaningful insights by tracking key metrics such as revenue performance, order trends, product performance, customer satisfaction, regional performance, and discount effectiveness.

**Business Need**

Leadership teams require timely and accurate insights to understand business performance, identify growth opportunities, and make informed strategic decisions. The current challenge is the lack of a centralized reporting solution that consolidates important KPIs into an executive-friendly view.

**Solution Approach**

This project will develop an executive KPI dashboard using data analysis, SQL, Tableau visualization, and AI-assisted project management practices.

The solution will provide stakeholders with:

- Centralized KPI visibility
- Faster access to business insights
- Improved understanding of product and customer trends
- Data-driven recommendations for strategic planning
